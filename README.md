# Rainfall-Based Flood Risk Visualization

## Overview
Analyzes year-wise rainfall trends for coastal Tamil Nadu 
with a focus on Chennai — identifying historically anomalous 
years that indicate elevated flood risk periods.

## Methodology
- Calculated historical average rainfall across all available years
- Defined high anomaly threshold as 125% of historical average
- Years exceeding threshold highlighted as elevated flood risk
- Focused on visualization-driven risk indication rather than 
  event-level flood prediction

## Key Finding
X years out of Y total years exceeded the 125% threshold — 
identifying specific high-risk periods in Chennai's rainfall 
history

## Tools
Python · Pandas · Matplotlib

## Files
- rainfall_project.ipynb — full analysis notebook
- rainfall.csv — source dataset# Rainfall-Based Flood Risk Visualization
